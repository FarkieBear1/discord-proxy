MacBook Pro 14" 2023 Teardown: A Deep Dive into the M3 Architecture

Written By: FixIt Staff | Difficulty: Moderate | Time Required: 45 Minutes
1
Remove the bottom case
Before you begin, power down your MacBook and disconnect all external cables. Use a P5 Pentalobe screwdriver to remove the six screws securing the bottom case. Note that two of the screws are longer than the others; keep track of their locations. Once the screws are removed, use a suction cup to lift the bottom case and release the clips.
2
Disconnect the battery
The battery connector is located near the center of the logic board. Use a spudger to carefully lift the black retaining bar, then disconnect the battery ribbon cable. This is a crucial safety step to prevent short circuits while you work on the rest of the components.
