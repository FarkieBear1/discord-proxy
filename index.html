General Hardware Protocols

🔋 Battery Replacement Protocol

  1. Preliminary Health Audit: Verify hardware failure via OS battery reports (`powercfg /batteryreport`) and cycle counts.
  2. Safe De-Energization: Power down completely, disconnect AC power, and remove all peripherals.
  3. ESD Isolation: Work on a grounded ESD mat and remove bottom cover fasteners.
  4. Primary Circuit Isolation: Disconnect the internal battery connector straight and parallel before touching logic board components.
  5. Fastener Extraction: Remove securing screws or pull stretch-release adhesive tabs safely.
  6. Post-Installation: Power on via AC adapter first to verify system charge recognition.

💻 Display Assembly Protocol

  1. Failure Isolation: Confirm fault originates in panel matrix or eDP cable using an external monitor.
  2. Power Shutdown: Shut down system and disconnect internal battery before touching display connectors.
  3. Hinge & Antenna Exposure: Detach hinge covers and unseat Wi-Fi/webcam ribbon cables.
  4. Lid Separation: Carefully unclip snap-fit bezels using non-marring plastic spudgers.
  5. Pre-Closure Test: Reconnect battery temporarily to verify pixel and backlight integrity.

💾 NVMe / Storage Protocol

  1. Interface Identification: Check form factor (M.2 2280 NVMe, SATA, etc.).
  2. Data Backup & Safety: Secure BitLocker recovery keys and disconnect internal battery power.
  3. Thermal Shield Removal: Lift away metallic heat shields, preserving thermal pads.
  4. Drive Extraction: Remove retention screw at a 30-degree angle and slide out.
  5. Validation: Run SMART health checks and read/write benchmarks post-install.
