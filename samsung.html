Firmware & Diagnostic Key Combinations

  • BIOS Setup: Tap F2 repeatedly on power-on.
  • One-Time Boot Menu: Tap F10 on startup.
  • Samsung Factory Recovery: Tap F4 on boot.

Power & Charging LED Diagnostics

  • Blinking Red Light: Non-compliant USB-C Power Delivery power supply.
  • Blinking Blue Light: System modern standby lockup state.

Samsung Galaxy Book Repair Tips

  1. Rubber Feet Extraction: Heat rubber feet with a thermal blower prior to lifting to avoid tearing adhesive padding.
  2. Zero Insertion Force (ZIF) Clips: Handle tiny ZIF ribbon clamps with plastic tools to prevent snapping retention bars.
