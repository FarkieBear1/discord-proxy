Technical Reference

Startup and firmware

TopicProcedure / notes
BIOS / UEFIDel during startup (common on MSI notebooks); some models use F2
Boot device selectionF11 on many MSI laptops
RecoveryF3 recovery on supported factory images; recovery partition must exist
Model identificationCheck underside model code and MSI support page before flashing firmware

Repair workflow

TopicProcedure / notes
Battery and powerShut down, disconnect AC, open according to model service instructions, disconnect internal battery before cables.
SSD and RAMCheck soldered vs socketed memory, M.2 slot generation and supported SSD dimensions before ordering.
ThermalsInspect fan operation and dust buildup; document thermal-pad thickness before replacing pads.
FirmwareUse exact model and board revision; maintain AC power and do not interrupt flashing.

Common faults

TopicProcedure / notes
No powerCheck adapter wattage, DC-in or USB-C PD compatibility, battery connection and charging indicators.
Black screenTest external display, brightness, RAM seating if serviceable and firmware recovery options.
High temperaturesReview fan curves, vents, heatsink contact and background load; compare idle/load temperatures.

Service note: Model, firmware version and region can change key combinations and hardware options. Verify the exact device service manual and back up important data before repairs.
