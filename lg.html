Laptop Product Families: Consumer vs. Business vs. Workstation

Model families and names change by year and region. Use the exact model number and service documentation when ordering parts or checking repairability.

GradeCommon product familiesTypical use / notes

Repair workflow

TopicProcedure / notes
BatteryDisconnect AC and internal battery before servicing; follow model-specific battery connector direction.
StorageCheck installed M.2 form factor, PCIe generation and available slot; some models provide multiple SSD slots.
MemoryMany thin gram models have soldered RAM; verify before promising upgrades.
DisplayThin panels and narrow bezels are fragile; avoid pressure and verify exact panel cable/pinout.

Troubleshooting

TopicProcedure / notes
No chargingConfirm compatible USB-C PD wattage and test charger/cable independently.
WirelessInstall LG-approved chipset and WLAN drivers for exact model.
ThermalsCheck intake clearance and fan operation; ultra-light chassis may throttle under sustained workloads.

Service note: Model, firmware version and region can change key combinations and hardware options. Verify the exact device service manual and back up important data before repairs.
