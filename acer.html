Firmware & Diagnostic Key Combinations

  • BIOS Setup: Tap F2 repeatedly on startup.
  • One-Time Boot Menu: Tap F12 on startup (Requires enabling "F12 Boot Menu" inside BIOS main tab first).
  • Factory Recovery: Hold Alt + F10 during startup splash screen.
  • Battery Disablement Pinhole: Disconnect AC adapter, press bottom pinhole button for 4 seconds, and pause 5 minutes before re-powering.

LED Fault Behavior & Diagnostics

  • Blinking Amber Power LED: Battery charge below critical state or charger voltage drop.
  • Blinking Blue LED: System in Modern Standby / Sleep state wake freeze.
  • No LED Output with AC Connected: Tripped input protection MOSFET or blown power rail inductor.

Acer Disassembly & Safety Switch

  1. Interlock Safety Switch: Modern Acer mainboards feature a mechanical micro-switch un-pressed by the base panel. If the bottom cover is loose, the laptop will not power on from battery.
  2. Hinge Reinforcement: Always unfasten Acer display hinges before lifting mainboard assemblies to prevent brass standoff tearing.
