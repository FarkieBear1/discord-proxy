Technical Reference

Startup and recovery

TopicProcedure / notes
Apple silicon startup optionsShut down; press and hold power until startup options appear.
Intel startup managerHold Option (⌥) while powering on.
Intel macOS RecoveryCommand (⌘) + R during startup; internet recovery availability varies.
Safe ModeApple silicon: select startup disk in startup options, hold Shift and choose Continue in Safe Mode; Intel: hold Shift at startup.
Apple DiagnosticsApple silicon: hold power for startup options, then Command-D; Intel: hold D at startup.

Hardware servicing

TopicProcedure / notes
BatteryUse model-specific Apple repair procedures; adhesive and battery handling present puncture and fire risks.
StorageApple silicon internal storage is generally not user-replaceable; confirm board configuration before planning repairs.
DisplaysReplacement and calibration requirements vary by model; True Tone and camera functions may require post-repair configuration.
Activation LockCheck Find My/Activation Lock ownership before erasing or transferring a Mac.

Troubleshooting

TopicProcedure / notes
PowerCheck USB-C/MagSafe adapter, cable, ports, and charging state; test with compatible known-good supply.
Storage healthDisk Utility First Aid checks file system structures; keep a backup before repairs.
BatterySystem Settings → Battery → Battery Health; System Information shows cycle count on supported models.
Firmware revive/restoreSome Macs support DFU revive/restore with a second Mac; restore can erase data.

Service note: Model, firmware version and region can change key combinations and hardware options. Verify the exact device service manual and back up important data before repairs.
