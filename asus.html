BIOS Keys, AMI POST Beep Codes, & Servicing Protocols

ASUS Repair Protocols

🔑 ASUS Firmware & Boot Keys

  • BIOS / UEFI Setup: Tap F2 or Delete repeatedly on startup.
  • One-Time Boot Menu: Tap Esc (or F8 on legacy ROG boards) on startup.
  • Hard CMOS Reset: Hold Power button for 40 seconds straight from off state to trigger EC reset.

🚨 ASUS / AMI BIOS POST Beep Codes

Format: Standard American Megatrends POST Audio Sequences

  • 1 Short Beep: System booted successfully / POST pass.
  • 1 Long, 2 Short Beeps: Memory / RAM detection failure or unseated SODIMM.
  • 1 Long, 3 Short Beeps: Display / Graphics card error (eDP ribbon issue).
  • 1 Long, 4 Short Beeps: Hardware component failure / CPU fan speed fault.

🔋 ROG & ZenBook Servicing Protocols

  1. Chassis Screw Tracking: Track Torx T5 screw lengths during disassembly to prevent puncture damage.
  2. Battery Safety Clip: Slide back the metal locking mechanism before unplugging battery header.
  3. Liquid Metal Caution: Modern ROG models use factory liquid metal; isolate thoroughly prior to heatsink removal.
