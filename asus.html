ASUS Hardware Reference

ASUS Startup Keys

  • BIOS Setup: Press F2 or Del immediately at boot.
  • Boot Menu: Press Esc or F8 (on desktop boards) for drive selection.
  • EZ Flash Firmware Update: Enter UEFI BIOS and navigate to Advanced Mode -> Tools -> ASUS EZ Flash 3 Utility.

Hard EC Reset (Hard Power Cycle)

To clear stuck power states or blank screen initialization issues on ROG/ZenBook models:

  1. Disconnect the AC adapter.
  2. Press and hold the Power button down continuously for 40 seconds.
  3. Plug the AC adapter back in and power on the system normally.
